Admissions 2026-27

The session starts in April. Admissions run from February to March, and seats in each class are limited.

    Call or message us

    Tell us your child’s age and which branch suits you. We’ll share timings and age criteria.

    Visit the school

    See the classrooms and courtyard, and meet the Principal and teachers.

  3. 3

    Settle in

    Complete the admission form. Your child’s first week is gentle, with shorter days if needed.

Classes

  • Playgroup

    First steps away from home: songs, sand, colours and lots of cuddles while little ones learn to settle in.

  • Nursery

    Rhymes, shapes, colour days and first scribbles, with a gentle routine that builds confidence.

  • LKG

    Letters, numbers and Hindi swar through play, plus stage time with Speak on Mic.

  • UKG

    Reading, writing and number work that gets children ready for Class 1, ending with Graduation Day.
