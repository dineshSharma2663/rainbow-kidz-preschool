Events and celebrations

Festivals with costumes and stories, trips, family days and the big milestones of the school year.

  • 7 April 2025

    Results and Awards Day

    Report cards and certificates handed out in front of a rainbow-decorated stage; proof of learning outcomes.

  • 25 May 2026

    Mother's Day

    Mothers join in: tilak welcome, a catwalk, dances and games in paper-flower decorated classrooms.

  • 17 November 2025

    Children's Day Magic Show

    Cartoon-themed balloon setup (Mickey, Doraemon), a live magic show in the courtyard and group photos by class.

  • 4 December 2025

    Picnic: Eco Adventure Camp

    Day trip to an eco adventure camp: climbing wall, bouncy castle, net crawl, potter demo and a meal together, all in school uniform.

  • 28 December 2024

    Annual Function: 15 Glorious Years

    Celebration of 15 years on a hired auditorium stage: costumes, group dances and guests welcomed with tilak.

  • 26 January 2026

    Republic Day (77th)

    Whole school gathers in the courtyard; children speak and perform in tricolour outfits.

  • 15 February 2026

    Mahashivratri: Shiv Barat

    Children dressed as Shiva, Parvati and the baraat walk in procession across campus, ending in a decorated stage tableau.

  • March 2026

    Admission Open 2026-27

    Children around campus hold up messages for their parents: 'Don't worry too much', 'Our teachers take care of us'.

  • 3 March 2026

    Holi

    Story of Prahlad, gulal play in the courtyard and colourful hand-print activities.

  • 24 March 2026

    Graduation Day 2026

    Graduation ceremony with speeches from the Principal and Director, kids' dances and medals.
