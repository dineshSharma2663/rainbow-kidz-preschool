Learning through play

Every week has a theme children can touch and say out loud: a colour day, a shape day, Hindi swar, a turn on the mic. Big ideas like safety and kindness come through stories and games.

  • Colour days (red, yellow, orange)
  • Shape days (circle, triangle)
  • Hindi swar and two-letter words
  • Alphabet activities
  • Standing and sleeping lines
  • Fruit matching
  • Speak on Mic
  • Ladder drill and hula-hoop in/out
  • Greetings and manners
  • Yoga
